# Supplied festival map: remote evidence check

Draft for the phone prototype. Checked October 31, 2026. An organizer-linked map was found, but this does not establish that it is the latest revision or verify physical temporary positions. The original supplied screenshot is preserved on the `research/orlando-sources` branch at `docs/research/assets/orlando-map-supplied.jpg` (SHA-256 `CD70B33DF1CFBDA01EF40784468E0122E96E2386F38EF98B21DD6A2FF53DAD8E`).

## What the evidence supports

- The [2026 event listing](https://www.campingworldstadium.com/events/detail/warped-tour-2026) and [festival's Orlando site](https://www.vanswarpedtourorlando.com/) identify the Camping World Stadium campus for November 14–15. This establishes venue and dates, not the detailed event layout.
- The supplied screenshot visually matches the artwork in the [October 6 Reddit post titled “Orlando 2026 Warped Tour Grounds Map”](https://www.reddit.com/r/warpedtour/comments/1wz81gc/orlando_2026_warped_tour_grounds_map/). We viewed the post's [underlying JPEG](https://i.redd.it/pxxv5d5dpvth1.jpeg) against the supplied screenshot: same labels and placement, with the screenshot adding a Reddit frame. The post title is a third-party attribution, not organizer provenance or proof this is the latest revision. A commenter questioned the left/right stage labeling; that is a report of uncertainty, not proof of a mislabel.
- The [organizer Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeR_PtOJj1p/) was inspected publicly in a browser. The post identifies `vanswarpedtour` and `vanswarpedtourfl`, shows a verified badge for `vanswarpedtour`, and its accessible text dates the image October 9, 2026. The inspected [screenshot](evidence/organizer-map-post.png) shows broadly the same layout, **but not the same labels**: the organizer post says **Club Wyndham Stage** and **Vans Stage**, while the supplied Reddit image says **Wyndham Stage** and **Vans Right Foot Stage**. This is organizer-linked evidence for the illustrated layout and labels as posted, not proof the organizer image is the latest revision or that its temporary features were installed as pictured. Do not silently substitute one set of names for the other.
- The stadium's [generic parking map](https://www.campingworldstadium.com/assets/doc/CampingWorld_ParkingMap_Generic-d4366feebe.pdf) shows the stadium and Church Street, Tampa Avenue, Norton Avenue, Long Street and State Road 408 in the wider area. The supplied schematic labels those same surroundings. The match is consistent with the campus; it does not establish exact alignment, entrances, festival barriers, stage locations, paths, travel distances or the image's vintage. The generic map predates this event and is not an event map.
- The session's browser inspection of [Google Maps for Camping World Stadium](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Camping+World+Stadium+Orlando+FL) showed the venue at 1 Citrus Bowl Place and surrounding Church Street, Rio Grande Avenue and the nearby lake. The map view was centered approximately at 28.5390417, -81.4027486; that is a view coordinate, not a verified stage position. This provides geographic context only. No pixel-to-geographic calibration was performed.

## Limits and new leads

- The [official Orlando FAQ](https://www.vanswarpedtourorlando.com/faq) says a festival map will be available on the site and official app. This promise does not authenticate a particular image. The [festival guide](https://www.vanswarpedtourorlando.com/guide) did not expose a festival map in the text retrieved in this check; absence from retrieved text does not prove none exists on the site, in images, or in the app.
- A community [2026 FAQ](https://www.reddit.com/r/warpedtour/comments/1x6noae/orlando_warped_2026_faq_day_of_info_what_to_bing/) links its “Fest Grounds Map” to the organizer Instagram post above. The first text-only fetch was throttled; the later browser inspection provided the evidence described above.
- The stadium's [event guide](https://www.campingworldstadium.com/plan-your-visit/vans-warped-tour-orlando-event-guide) still displays November 15–16 and mixed material referencing Rolling Loud and a three-day parking pass. Treat it as stale or mixed-edition material; its proposed entrance and map placeholder cannot verify 2026 operations.
- The [stadium construction notice](https://www.campingworldstadium.com/construction) lists road closures and work continuing into 2027. It reinforces the need to check current event-specific access; it does not show the festival layout or establish that any particular gate is usable.

## Prototype and release implication

Label the supplied image as a **user-supplied reference with differences from the October 9 organizer post**. The organizer-post image can support a clearly attributed discussion of where labels appear on that image; it cannot establish GPS positions or guarantee the current festival layout. Do not assert physically verified stage pins, real-time positioning, navigable or accessible routes, walking times, or verified festival entrance locations. Resolve the label differences and recheck the current organizer map and revision before offering confident stage focus in a release. The separately tracked event-data release owns that later verification. No physical visit, street-level inspection, official app inspection, or live event validation occurred in this check.
