# Orlando festival companion

The language of planning and attending Warped Tour Orlando 2026, including personal choices and shared event information.

## Language

**Appearance**:
A particular published musical performance by an artist, including main-stage, acoustic, and secondary-stage performances. Separate appearances by the same artist remain distinct.
_Avoid_: Artist used to mean a specific performance

**Interest**:
A person's desire to see an artist or appearance, including its priority, whether or not their attendance choices can accommodate it.
_Avoid_: Attendance commitment

**Attendance choice**:
A person's intended participation in an appearance: all, a selected portion, or none. Changing attendance does not erase their underlying interest.
_Avoid_: Favorite used to mean confirmed attendance

**My plan**:
A person's saved interests and attendance choices for the festival.
_Avoid_: Shared timetable

**Timetable**:
The event information assigning appearances to days, times, and stages. It is distinct from each person's interests and attendance choices.
_Avoid_: My plan

**Schedule-import code**:
A shareable code containing a timetable revision that a person can bring into their plan while preserving personal interests and attendance choices.
_Avoid_: Friend-plan code, postcode

**Published revision**:
A particular release of shared event information approved by the event-data publisher. It is distinct from a person's edits and from the organizer's original source material.
_Avoid_: Official schedule when referring to this app's own release

**Personal edit**:
A person's own correction to event information, separate from the published revision. It does not change the information other festivalgoers receive.
_Avoid_: Published correction

**Source check**:
A review of specified event information against its identified source at a recorded time. It does not imply that every appearance, time, stage, or map location is known.
_Avoid_: Download time, complete timetable

**Clash**:
An overlap between appearances in a person's plan. Missing times prevent checking an overlap; insufficient travel time is a separate warning.
_Avoid_: Conflict-free when information is missing

**Planned position**:
A person's expected location based on a shared plan, rather than an observation of their current location.
_Avoid_: Live location

**Manual check-in**:
A person's explicitly recorded location at a stated time. It does not establish their current location after that time.
_Avoid_: Live tracking
